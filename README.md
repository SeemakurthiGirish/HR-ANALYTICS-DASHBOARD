# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI**, designed to help HR teams and business leaders track employee attrition, workforce demographics, and satisfaction trends across the organization.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This dashboard analyzes a workforce of **1,470 employees**, providing a 360° view of attrition patterns, job satisfaction, and demographic breakdowns. It enables HR stakeholders to identify high-risk departments, roles, and employee segments driving turnover — supporting data-driven retention strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, DAX measures, and visualization
- **DAX** – Custom calculated measures (Attrition Rate, Avg Age, Avg Monthly Pay, etc.)
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Employees by Age Group & Gender** – Distribution across age brackets, split by gender
- **Attrition by Age** – Trend line showing attrition concentration around ages 28–34
- **Department-wise Attrition** – Donut chart comparing R&D, Sales, and HR attrition share
- **Attrition Rate by Job Role** – Highlights Sales Representatives (39.76%) as the highest-risk role
- **Job Satisfaction Rating Matrix** – Cross-tab of satisfaction scores (1–4) by job role
- **Attrition by Education Field** – Funnel chart showing Life Sciences and Medical as top contributing fields
- **Interactive Slicers** – Filter by Gender, Marital Status, Business Travel frequency, and Education level

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Sales Representatives have the **highest attrition rate at 39.76%**, far above other roles
- Attrition peaks sharply in the **25–34 age group**
- **R&D department** accounts for the largest share of attrition (56.12%)
- Employees with **Life Sciences and Medical** backgrounds show the highest attrition counts
